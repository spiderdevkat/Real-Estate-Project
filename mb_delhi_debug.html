Located approx. 35 km away from the capital of India Delhi, Gurgaon is known as a Millennium City. If we talk about its real estate market the city comes with urban paradise that is well-connected via Delhi Metro. With a huge number of migrant and newcomers in this city, there is a high demand for rented homes in Gurgaon. Being the corporate destination, there are a huge number of residential projects and commercial spaces in Gurgaon spread across top localities of the city. From leading Indian automobile manufacturer Maruti Suzuki India Limited plant to Bharti Airtel Ltd. you will find all the renowned corporate sector offices in the tall and magnificent building of Gurgaon. There is a wide variety of real estate project that gives this city a different look like corporate spaces, grand hotels, multi-storey residential apartments and luxurious bungalows in Gurgaon. If you are one of those who are looking for houses for rent or sale in Gurgaon you will be happy to know that the entire city is currently developing and there are lots of residential projects developed or under construction by many renowned builders of North India. If you are working beyond the highlighted commercial hubs of Gurgaon like Manesar, Bhiwadi or Dharuhera and searching for your perfect home in Gurgaon then you can consider Bhiwadi Sector 15 where social infrastructure and house for rent & sale are easily available at a very nominal price.
